# Limitations And Extensions

This section separates limitations of the current experiments from limitations of the simulation engine itself. It also lists concrete extensions that would strengthen future analysis or broaden the kinds of models the application can express.

- [Engine limitations and extensions](Engine-Limitations-and-Extensions): neighborhood, state, randomness, automation, metrics, and browser/GPU constraints.
- [References](References): project documentation, comparison models, foundational cellular-automata and epidemic sources, and analysis tools.
