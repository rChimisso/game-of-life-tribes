# References

Organized lists of reference material used as inspiration or comparison during the develpment of this project.

## Cellular Automata

- [Life-like cellular automaton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life-like_cellular_automaton), Wikipedia: overview of Moore-neighborhood outer-totalistic `B/S` rules and common generalizations.
- [Cellular Automata](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/cellular-automata/), Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy: historical and conceptual overview, including Conway's Game of Life.
- [LifeWiki: Life-like cellular automata](https://conwaylife.com/wiki/Outer-totalistic): `B/S` rulestring notation and Life-like rule reference.
- [Colorful Life](https://github.com/jaxry/colorful-life), by jaxry: full-color Game of Life implementation using WebGL in the browser.
- [The Colorful Game of Life](https://github.com/adam-zheleznyak/colorful-life), by adam-zheleznyak: colorized Conway's Game of Life implementation with mutation and alternative ruleset experiments.

## NetLogo And Agent-Based Models

- Wilensky, U. (1999), [NetLogo](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/), Center for Connected Learning and Computer-Based Modeling, Northwestern University.
- Wilensky, U. (1997), [NetLogo Fire model](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/models/Fire), Center for Connected Learning and Computer-Based Modeling, Northwestern University.
- [BehaviorSpace](https://docs.netlogo.org/behaviorspace), NetLogo User Manual: parameter sweeps, repetitions, seeds, stopping conditions, and headless experiments.
- Li, J. and Wilensky, U. (2009), [NetLogo Sugarscape 1 Immediate Growback model](https://ccl.northwestern.edu/netlogo/models/Sugarscape1ImmediateGrowback), Center for Connected Learning and Computer-Based Modeling, Northwestern University.
- Epstein, J. M. and Axtell, R. (1996), [*Growing Artificial Societies: Social Science from the Bottom Up*](https://www.brookings.edu/books/growing-artificial-societies/), Brookings Institution Press and MIT Press.

## Epidemic Models

- Hethcote, H. W. (2000), ["The Mathematics of Infectious Diseases"](https://doi.org/10.1137/S0036144500371907), *SIAM Review* 42(4):599–653.
- Wolff, M. (2020), ["On Build-up of Epidemiologic Models—Development of a SEI³RSD Model for the Spread of SARS-CoV-2"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7646042/), *ZAMM* 100:e202000230, [DOI 10.1002/zamm.202000230](https://doi.org/10.1002/zamm.202000230).
- Joo, J. and Lebowitz, J. L. (2004), ["Pair Approximation of the Stochastic Susceptible-Infected-Recovered-Susceptible Epidemic Model on the Hypercubic Lattice"](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15524594/), *Physical Review E* 70:036114, [DOI 10.1103/PhysRevE.70.036114](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.70.036114).
- van Ballegooijen, W. M. and Boerlijst, M. C. (2004), ["Emergent Trade-offs and Selection for Outbreak Frequency in Spatial Epidemics"](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC539754/), *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences* 101(52):18246–18250, [DOI 10.1073/pnas.0405682101](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0405682101).
